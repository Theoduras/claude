How Velvt works

If the answer isn't here, write to us — a person reads that address.

Matching

Why is there no one to swipe through?

There deliberately isn't a stack of profiles. You start a search saying what you're looking for, and you're paired with someone whose search matches yours — while you're both actually there. How a match works walks through it.

How long does a match last?

You get {{ reveal_seconds }} seconds to see who it is, then {{ chat_minutes }} minutes to talk. After that you both choose whether to continue. If you both say yes the chat stays open for good and photos unlock. If either of you doesn't, it ends there.

What happens if the other person doesn't answer?

Nothing you have to manage. If a decision isn't made, the match closes on its own and neither of you is left with it sitting in a list.

Why is nobody matching my search?

You're only paired with someone searching at the same time, so it depends on who is looking right now. The waiting screen shows each of your filters as a tag you can tap to change, with a real count of how many people that change would reach. If nothing fits for a while, it offers to drop the optional filters in one go.

Your profile

What do I need before I can search?

A name, your age, your gender, who you're looking for, and one photo. Everything else is optional and makes you easier to match — the strength meter on your profile shows what's left.

Who can see my photos?

Only you, until a match becomes permanent — that is, until you have both chosen to continue. Nobody browsing can see them, because there is no browsing.

Safety and privacy

How do I report or block someone?

Every chat and every profile has a report link, and reporting lets you block at the same time. Blocking is instant and silent: they aren't told, and the two of you are never matched again. See staying safe.

Someone asked me for money.

Report them, and don't send anything. This is the most common way people are defrauded on dating apps and the story is always urgent and always sympathetic. Nobody you have just met needs your money.

Can I get my data, or delete my account?

Both, from settings, without asking anyone. Export downloads everything we hold about you. Deletion is scheduled rather than instant so you can change your mind, and after that your profile and photos are destroyed — messages the other person still has stay in their conversation, no longer attached to you.

I've forgotten my password.

Use the reset link on the sign-in page. Resetting signs you out everywhere else, which is the point of it.
