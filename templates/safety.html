Safety

Looking after yourself on Velvt

Most people here are exactly who they say they are. These are the habits that keep the rest from mattering.

Before you meet

  • Meet somewhere public the first time, and get yourself there and back.
  • Tell a friend where you're going and when you expect to be home.
  • A short video call first tells you more than any number of messages.
  • Keep chatting on Velvt until you're comfortable. Someone pushing hard to move to another app early is a pattern worth noticing.

Money is the red flag

Nobody you have just met needs your money. Requests for a loan, a gift card, a crypto investment, help with a customs fee, or a plane ticket are the most common way people are defrauded on dating apps — and the story is always urgent and always sympathetic. Reported losses to romance scams ran to over a billion dollars in the US alone in the first nine months of 2025.

If money comes up, stop and report them. You will not be the first, and reporting is what stops them reaching the next person.

Other things worth reporting

  • Anyone who seems to be under 18
  • Sexual messages or images you didn't ask for
  • Threats, abuse, or someone who won't take no for an answer
  • Photos that clearly belong to someone else

Every profile and every chat has a report button. Blocking is instant and silent — they aren't told, and you'll never be matched again.

If something has happened

If you're in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services first — 112 across the EU. Then tell us at {{ support_email }} so we can act on the account.
