Before you start

How a match
works here.

Velvt doesn't hand you a stack of profiles to swipe. You search, and you're paired the moment someone is looking for you too.

  1. 1

    You both get {{ reveal_seconds }} seconds

    When you're paired, a card tells you who it is and what you have in common. Photos stay hidden for now.

  2. 2

    Then {{ chat_minutes }} minutes to talk

    A real conversation on a clock. Long enough to find out whether there's anything there, short enough that neither of you is stuck being polite.

  3. 3

    You both decide

    Continue, and the chat stays open and photos unlock. If either of you doesn't, it ends there — no explanation owed, nothing lingering in a list.

You can report or block anyone, at any point, from the chat or their profile. Staying safe is worth two minutes before you meet anyone.
